Description

A professional network connecting OOXME with companies and project owners through trusted business relationships. Partners share suitable opportunities and connect clients with our team, while OOXME handles evaluation, presentation, commercial discussions, and contracting.

Applications open: 1 October 2026

Strong Relations

Connect with business owners, project owners, managers, and decision-makers.

Related Field

Work in, or have strong connections to, engineering, architecture, construction, or related sectors.

OOXME Commitment

Follow OOXME’s approved referral process and professional communication standards.

25% Commission

Available for clients referred through you when they sign a contract with OOXME.
